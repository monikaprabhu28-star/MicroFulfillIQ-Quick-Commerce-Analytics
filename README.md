# MicroFulfillIQ — Quick Commerce Dark Store Efficiency & Fulfillment Intelligence Dashboard

## Project Overview

MicroFulfillIQ is a business analytics dashboard developed to analyze quick-commerce dark store operations, fulfillment efficiency, inventory performance, customer experience, and marketing performance.

The project uses a realistic quick-commerce dataset and Microsoft Power BI to transform operational data into interactive dashboards, key performance indicators, business insights, and actionable recommendations.

## Industry

**Quick Commerce / E-commerce / Supply Chain & Logistics**

## Business Problem

Quick-commerce businesses need to fulfill customer orders rapidly while maintaining product availability, efficient delivery operations, customer satisfaction, and effective marketing performance.

Monitoring these areas separately can make it difficult for management to identify operational inefficiencies, delivery issues, inventory problems, and customer experience challenges.

MicroFulfillIQ provides a centralized analytical dashboard that enables management to monitor key business and operational metrics and support data-driven decision-making.

## Objectives

* Analyze overall order and revenue performance.
* Monitor customer and order trends.
* Evaluate order fulfillment performance.
* Analyze delivery time and delivery delays.
* Monitor inventory received and damaged stock.
* Analyze customer ratings and feedback.
* Evaluate marketing spending, revenue, and return on investment.
* Identify operational and customer-experience improvement areas.
* Provide actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Key Business Insights

1. The business records approximately **1.06K orders** and **2.36M in revenue**, providing a consolidated view of overall business performance.

2. The **69.8% on-time delivery rate** indicates that delivery reliability is an important area for operational improvement.

3. The **30.2% delay rate** and **320 delayed orders** highlight the need to investigate delivery bottlenecks and recurring delay causes.

4. Customer satisfaction is approximately **3.38**, indicating opportunities to improve the overall customer experience.

5. Approximately **29K units of stock were received**, while around **1K units were recorded as damaged**, highlighting the importance of inventory quality monitoring.

6. Marketing generated approximately **15.52M in revenue** against **7.89M in marketing spend**, with an average ROAS of **1.97**.

7. The dashboard tracks **2K total quantity sold** across **268 products**, providing visibility into product and sales performance.

## Recommendations

* Improve delivery planning and fulfillment processes to increase the on-time delivery rate.
* Investigate recurring causes of delivery delays and address operational bottlenecks.
* Monitor damaged inventory by product category.
* Improve inventory handling and storage practices.
* Analyze customer feedback regularly to identify service-quality issues.
* Optimize marketing campaigns based on revenue contribution and ROAS.
* Focus on high-performing products and marketing channels.
* Monitor product quantity and category performance to support inventory and sales decisions.
* Use the dashboard regularly to monitor operational performance and support management decisions.
